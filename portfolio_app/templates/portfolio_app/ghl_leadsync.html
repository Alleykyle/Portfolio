Lead Sync:
GoHighLevel to Google Sheets

When a contact is tagged in GoHighLevel, their details are sent to a Google Sheet automatically. Built twice, in Zapier and in Make, to compare the two tools.

WebhooksZapierMake FiltersGoogle Sheets

⚠️

The problem

Many businesses keep their leads in a spreadsheet as well as their CRM. Copying contacts by hand is slow, and rows get missed or mistyped.

🛠️

What I built

A GoHighLevel workflow sends the contact's name, email, and phone to a webhook. Zapier and Make each filter out incomplete records and add the rest as a new row.

✅

The result

Every tagged lead appears in the sheet within seconds, and records without an email or phone number are skipped.

ℹ️ Built and tested with fictional contact data in a GoHighLevel sandbox, using a Zapier trial and Make's free plan. The Zap and the scenario were tested but left switched off, because the GoHighLevel webhook is a premium action billed per execution.
