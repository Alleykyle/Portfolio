The problem

When onboarding is manual, welcome emails go out late, intake forms get forgotten, and nobody knows which clients are waiting on what.

🛠️

What I built

A workflow that starts when a client is tagged, sends a welcome email and intake form, tracks a five-stage pipeline, and checks whether the form was completed.

✅

The result

Every client gets the same start. Completed forms move the client forward and alert the team, and everyone else gets a reminder.

ℹ️ Built and tested in a GoHighLevel sandbox where outbound email is restricted, so workflow logic was verified through execution logs. Task assignment is not included, because the sandbox has no assignable staff user.
