The problem

Dental clinics lose leads when follow-up is manual. Patients inquire, nobody replies in time, and they book elsewhere.

🛠️

What I built

A booking form, a four-stage pipeline, and a workflow that confirms, notifies the team, tags the lead, and follows up.

✅

The result

Every inquiry is tracked from first contact. Only leads who haven't booked receive the reminder.

ℹ️ Built and tested in a GoHighLevel sandbox where outbound email is restricted. Workflow logic was verified through execution logs.
